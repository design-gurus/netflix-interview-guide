# Netflix Interview Question Bank

This page lists 54 practice questions for the Netflix loop: 24 coding, 16 system design, and 14 behavioral. The system design and behavioral questions are commonly reported for Netflix on DesignGurus.io pages. The coding questions are course problems chosen to cover the topics that candidates report, and each one links to its lesson.

## Behavioral (14)

41. What did you take away from our culture memo? What do you disagree with?
42. Why Netflix, and why now?
43. What does "freedom and responsibility" mean to you in practice?
44. How do you feel about the keeper test?
45. Tell me about the hardest feedback you have given someone. How did you deliver it?
46. Tell me about feedback that was hard to hear. What did you do with it?
47. Describe a time you disagreed with your manager and said so. What happened?
48. Tell me about a mistake of your own that you reported before anyone else found it.
49. Tell me about a significant decision you made without asking permission.
50. Tell me about a decision you made with incomplete information. Why did you not escalate?
51. Describe a time you spent company money as carefully as your own.
52. Tell me about a time you helped another team at a cost to your own goals.
53. Describe an unpopular position you took. Did you change anyone's mind?
54. Tell me about a time you stopped your own project because stopping was correct.
